### Travel information

- **By public transport:** The practice is centrally located in the Old Town and easy to reach by public transport.
- **By car:** Several car parks are available in the Old Town. The immediate surroundings are mostly paid parking zones.
- **Accessibility:** The practice entrance requires climbing **stairs**.

Sessions are also available via **video call** on request.
