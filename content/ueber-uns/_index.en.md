## Our Approach

We work systemically and constructively – meaning we always view people within their relational context, considering the interplay between people, expectations and experiences. Our stance is solution-focused and resource-oriented.

In our work we value:

- **Confidentiality** – What is discussed in therapy stays between us.
- **Respect** – We meet you without blame and without judgement.
- **Solution focus** – We look at resources and possibilities, not just problems.
- **Openness** – We work with people of all ages, gender identities and relationship models.
