Alongside our therapeutic work, we offer workshops in small groups throughout 2026. Groups are intentionally kept small so that we can work intensively and personally.

---

## Workshop for Women

**Self-determination, sensuality and vibrant femininity**

In this workshop we explore together topics such as self-love, body awareness, relationship design, desire and longing – through conversation and gentle breathing exercises.

The workshop is open to women of all ages and life situations.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Date** | Wednesday, 4 March 2026, 5–7 pm |
| **Cost** | €90 per participant |
| **Group size** | 6–8 women |
| **Language** | German |

## Workshop for Men

**Self-determination, strength and sensitivity**

Men reflect on their role, celebrate their strengths and learn to experience sensitivity as a resource – balancing power with empathy. Topics include emotional development, relationship dynamics and authentic masculine identity.

| **Date** | Wednesday, 15 April 2026, 5–7 pm |
| **Cost** | €90 per participant |
| **Group size** | 6–8 men |
| **Language** | German |

## Couples Workshop

**Relationship maintenance, intimacy and lasting satisfaction**

Based on the research of John Gottman, we work together on eight central themes: trust, conflict, sexuality, finances, family, enjoyment, personal growth and shared aspirations.

|---|---|

| **Cost** | €400 per couple |
| **Group size** | 3–4 couples |
